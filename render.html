About this study

Approximate geometry from the supplied plans and listing photos. Light is traced through the whole enclosed house and garden, including reflected sky light. Pause to let grain settle.

Clear skies use Blender’s Nishita atmospheric scattering with a 0.53° sun disk. Both renderers use the same HDR pixels. Overcast follows a CIE distribution. These are weather scenarios, not measurements. Exposure stays fixed when moving.

Window size, tree crowns, materials, roof shape, and furnishing positions are estimates. Glass includes Fresnel reflections and refraction; the transmission percentages are estimated normal-incidence values. The basement is omitted. Floor buttons bridge the unmodeled stair flight.
